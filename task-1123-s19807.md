## Why

- **The bug:** `dsl_dir_tempreserve_impl()` lets the estimated usage of a dsl_dir run 1/32 past its quota. At the root, the quota is the pool's limit for writes (`ZFS_SPACE_CHECK_NORMAL`), and the slop space above it belongs to other levels: half of it to `RESERVED` (`zfs set`, and on master the netfree transactions of `rm`), a quarter to `EXTRA_RESERVED` (`zfs destroy`). With the default `spa_asize_inflation` of 24 the real overshoot is about a 24th of the extension; with `spa_asize_inflation=1` it is the whole extension. On a 3 GiB file vdev pool the extension is 88 MiB and the RESERVED level is 64 MiB past the limit, so after writes fill the pool, `zfs set` and `rm` fail with ENOSPC.
- **The fix** (commit `a77321b9923`, `module/zfs/dsl_dir.c` only): at the root, the extension is capped at half the way to the next level (32 MiB of estimate here for writes).
- **Already measured (TASK-1122-F27, VM 106, release builds):** on this pool shape at `spa_asize_inflation=1`, writes stopped at least 85.4 MiB past the limit without the fix and at most 30.8 MiB with it (trees that also had #19807's other two commits, which do not change how writes are admitted).
- **The test** `enospc_inflation` (no_space group): pool on a 3 GiB file vdev (ashift 12, compression and atime off), two 16 MiB files, `spa_asize_inflation=1` while one file is written until ENOSPC, then the old value back and two `zpool sync -f`; then `zfs set user:inflation=1` and `rm` of one 16 MiB file must succeed. It logs `Writes stopped N MiB past the writes' limit; the level of zfs set is 64 MiB past it`.
- **Not needed here:** #19807 without this commit (`free-on-full-pool-v-next`, `006df1d1958`) is the exact tree F27 ran as arm B (`enospc_free` 3/3 PASS); it needs no VM run.
