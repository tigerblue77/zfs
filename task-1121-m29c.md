## Why

M-29: since `be1e991a1a8` ("Allow and prefer special vdevs as ZIL", 2.4.0), a write of at least `zfs_immediate_write_sz` (32 KiB) that is not committed at once is logged `WR_INDIRECT` whatever its size relative to the block. On a pool with neither log nor special vdevs, as the owner's host, `zil_commit()` then has `dmu_sync()` write every block such a write touches, in full, to the normal vdevs: a guest write of 512 KiB into a 1 MiB volblock becomes a 1 MiB synchronous write at the next flush. The code variant, `zil-indirect-half-block-2.4.4` `d81d76d6f07` (2.4.4 plus one commit, unsigned), adds `zil_indirect_min_pct` (default 0, the current behaviour): on such a pool, each record of an indirect write that covers less than that percentage of its block carries its data in the log instead (`WR_COPIED` or `WR_NEED_COPY`). On the rig's load in userspace (libzpool, no disks, no time), `zil_indirect_min_pct=50` wrote 41 % more bytes at the flushes and 3.3 times the leaf writes of 2.4.4; `zfs_immediate_write_sz=2097152` (kit v2's arm P1I, every write copied) 5 % fewer bytes and 5.4 times the writes; on random 64 KiB partial writes the variant cut the flush bytes by 94 %, which is its upstream case. Whether the host's flushes get faster is a question of time on disks, which this task answers on the rig, to a decision fixed before the runs.

## Written before the run

**Validity of a run** (kit v2's `hr-report.py`, used by `m29c-report.py`): the guest ended (`END`, no `ERROR`); the disks were in model (models 1); completions more than 2 ms late at most 1 % and no store error (V1); no request refused during the window; the run's `arm.txt` shows the arm's tree (srcversion loaded = built = the stamp's), every parameter the arm writes, blk-wbt at 75000 and `ncq_prio` 0 on all five disks; no marked request (this tree has no `zfs_vdev_disk_rt_mask`). Only valid runs count.

**The decision** (M-29's rule, `m29c-report.py`): D1 = M29C-50 against M29C-0. In each pass where both ran validly, r = guest fsync p99 of M29C-50 / guest fsync p99 of M29C-0 (each raised to 1 ms); g = the geometric mean of r over those passes. The spread s = the highest over the lowest guest fsync p99 of the calibrated point's three calibration runs, `G/<point>`, `C1/<point>` and `C2/<point>`, in the "## Runs" table of TASK-1121-HRIG2's `/var/log/task-1121-hrig2/calib.md` (at least two of them). **KEEP** when g < 1 / s (the variant's flush p99 beats the default's by more than the calibration's own spread) and there is no W1 loss (no pass where M29C-0's run holds W1, guest media MB/s at least 0.9 of the offered 50, and M29C-50's does not); otherwise **CLOSE** (the host variant is closed as measured, random partial writes stay its upstream case). **NONE** with fewer than 3 such passes, or without a spread: then main decides from the numbers.

**Shown, not deciding**: D1 also with kit v2's comparison rule (VQ1's: better if g <= 0.97 and the sign-flip p <= 0.05, which needs all six pairs on one side) and its lead verdict (primary: fsync p99, fsync p50, syncq_wait write p99; guards: guest read p99, disk_wait read p99, guest MB/s, txg sync mean; HELPS, TRADE-OFF, HURTS or NO EFFECT SHOWN), D2 = M29C-P1I against M29C-0 the same way, D3 = M29C-50 against M29C-P1I (info: what moved), and each arm's own fsync p99 spread over the passes.
