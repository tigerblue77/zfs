## Why

- **R19882P's stress pass** (#19932 at ddb6217, 4096): 4 of 5 iterations lost one 100K block in round 3. None straddles P. Each lost block (DVA asize `0x32800`, 404 sectors: the 6-wide allocation, birth txg 13 or 17) was in the old layout above round 2's P, reflowed before round 3, and in the new layout below round 3's P.
- **Each round's damage alone is within parity.** In round 2, dev-0..2 were zeroed over the block's whole old location (for example child MiB 37.79 to 37.83, inside `[34, 124)`): 3 children, one layout. In round 3, over its whole new location (32.40 to 32.42, inside `[0, 35)`): again 3 children, one layout. Round 2's scrub reported 0 errors. So round 3 can lose the block only if round 2's damage was still in the copy the reflow made, that is, if round 2's repair of the block was lost.
- **The suspect:** a raidz read during an expansion takes the expansion rangelock over `io_size`, the block's psize (200 sectors here), not over the 401 sectors its map covers. The reflow can copy the unlocked 201-sector tail while the scrub's read and repairs are in flight. The repairs then land at the old location only, and the damaged copy becomes the block's data. #19864 locks the whole allocation. It is open and is not in master or in #19932. Earlier evidence: TASK-1117-RX2 ran the test's order with 4 KiB chunks on master + #19864 for 30 rounds; every one of its 18 losses was the round's block at P, none was a block of an earlier round.
- **This task:** with #19932 alone, the callback should find, in each lost block's new location, zeroed sectors on dev-3..6 (round 2's damage, copied by the reflow) only in the unlocked tail. With #19864 added, the stress failures should disappear. Another VM runs #19932 and master at 4096 with the older callback at the same time; this task does not depend on them.

## The callback

The test's `log_fail` runs the scripts in `TESTFAIL_CALLBACKS` before the test's cleanup, as root, with the pool imported. This task's callback reads only. It prints, each line prefixed with `r19882-cb`:
- `P:` the last `raidz_reflow_info` lines of `zfs_dbgmsg` (the last one is the P of the failing round's import);
- `H:` the `zpool history -il` lines of the attach and of `raidz vdev expansion started`, with their txg;
- `E:` the `errors:` section of `zpool status -v testpool`;
- per listed file, `F:` path, dataset and object, and `B:` its `L0` lines from `zdb -e -p <devdir> -ddddd` (DVA `0:<offset>:<asize>`, sizes `<L>L/<P>P`, birth `B=`);
- per `L0` block (at most 16 per file): one `S:` line, then `M` and `O` lines, 60 sectors each, one character per sector `j` of the block's map (`j` from 0 to `map - 1`, sector `x` = DVA offset / 512 + `j`).

`M`, the block's new location (child `x % 7`, data-area sector `x / 7`):
- `3`: zero on dev-0..2 (round 3's damage, expected on every such sector);
- `!`: not zero on dev-0..2;
- `.`: not zero on dev-3..6;
- `L`: zero on dev-3..6 where the old layout put the sector on dev-0..2 (`x % 6 < 3`): earlier damage the reflow copied;
- `X`: zero on dev-3..6 anywhere else (no damage explains it).

`O`, the old location (child `x % 6`, sector `x / 6`): `z` zero, `=` same bytes as the new location, `d` different.

`S:` fields:
- `off` (in sectors), `map` (sectors in the raidz map), `alloc` (DVA asize in sectors), `w6=1` (the asize is the 6-wide one), `lock` (sectors under the read lock without #19864: psize / 512);
- `P` and `layout` (`new` if the block lies wholly below P);
- `r3=a/b` (`3` among the sectors on dev-0..2), `L`, `X`, `Lj=first..last` (the `j` range of the `L` sectors), `Linlock` (`L` sectors with `j < lock`);
- `Lold0` (`L` sectors whose old location is zero), `rows>3` and `maxrow` (rows of 6 sectors with more than 3 zero sectors; the most in one row);
- `ctrl=a/b` (never-damaged sectors whose old and new copies are equal), `unread` (sectors it could not read; 0 expected).

**How to read it** (for a lost block with `layout=new`):

| finding | meaning |
|---|---|
| `L` > 0, `Linlock=0`, `X=0` | round 2's repair was lost in the unlocked tail only: the gap #19864 closes |
| `L` > 0 and `Linlock` > 0 | some of the locked head was left unrepaired too: a lost-repair path #19864 does not cover |
| `L=0`, `X=0`, `rows>3=0` | the damage was within parity and the block was still lost: a reconstruction bug |
| `X` > 0, `!` or `?` in `M` | none of these; quote it as it is |

- `ctrl` decides whether `O` and `Lold0` mean anything. `ctrl=b/b`: the old copies are intact, and `Lold0=0` says the repair reached the old location after the reflow had copied the sector, `Lold0=L` that it never reached it.
- Below `b/b`, the reflow has overwritten the old location since round 3's import; ignore `O` and `Lold0` and say so.
