## Why

TASK-1121-L2D (your report of 22/11 15:11Z) ran version 1 (`aba3b50c9be`): `trim_l2arc_export` FAILED 3 of 3, `zpool remove testpool /var/tmp/trim-vdev2` did not return within 300 s and returned once the test restored the TRIM tunables. Stacks at 300 s: `zpool` in `l2arc_dev_feed_stop` from `spa_vdev_remove`; `l2arc_testpool_` in `cv_wait_common` from `vdev_trim_ranges` / `vdev_trim_simple` / `l2arc_evict` / `l2arc_feed_thread`.

The cause, now confirmed in user space (below): `l2arc_dev_feed_stop()` took `l2ad_feed_thr_lock` before setting `l2ad_thread_exit`. The feed thread holds that lock for a whole feed pass and, when a pass outlasts its interval, on into the next one: `cv_timedwait()` returns at once without dropping the mutex once its deadline has passed (SPL on Linux and FreeBSD, and libspl). With 512-byte TRIMs issued one at a time, every pass trims about 63 MiB, a quarter of the cache device (about 129 000 TRIMs, some 10 s at your 11-13k TRIMs/s), so the passes ran back to back for as long as the ARC held data to write, and the stop request could not even be posted. It was not a deadlock: the remover waited without holding the config lock.

Version 2 (the same branch name with `-v2`, both commits rewritten on the same base):

- fix (commit 1, `e488c9ac93b`): as before, `l2arc_spa_feed_stop()` / `l2arc_vdev_feed_stop()` stop the feed threads in `spa_export_common()`, `spa_unload()` and `spa_vdev_remove()` before the config lock is taken as writer. New: `l2arc_dev_feed_stop()` sets `l2ad_thread_exit` before taking `l2ad_feed_thr_lock`; `vdev_trim_simple()` takes a `const boolean_t *stop`, and `vdev_trim_range()` ends a simple TRIM before its next TRIM I/O once it is set; `l2arc_evict()` passes `&dev->l2ad_thread_exit`. The range is evicted all the same, as when a TRIM fails, and the rest of the pass (its writes, at most 64 MiB here) records the evict offset in the device header; then the thread exits. Manual, automatic and whole-device L2ARC TRIMs are unchanged.
- test (commit 2, `60ce5b8fb10`): `trim_l2arc_export` as before (pool on `/var/tmp/trim-vdev1`, 1 GiB, cache device `/var/tmp/trim-vdev2`, 256 MiB; `l2arc_trim_ahead=1`, `l2arc_write_max=64M`, `l2arc_dwpd_limit=0`; 768 MiB written with fio and read until the feed thread trims; then `zfs_trim_extent_bytes_max=512`, `zfs_trim_queue_limit=1`, at least 1000 TRIMs a second, and `zpool remove`, `zpool export`, `zpool destroy` in turn, each in the background), but each command now has 60 s instead of 300 s.
