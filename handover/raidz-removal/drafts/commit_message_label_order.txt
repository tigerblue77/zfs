zpool remove: erase the labels once the hole is on disk

spa_vdev_remove_empty() erased the labels of the removed vdev before
the txg that writes the hole had synced.  An import that scans the
devices ignores an erased label, so a crash in between left a pool
whose config still has the vdev and whose devices no longer show it.
The pool then imports only while the paths recorded in its config
still lead to the devices.

Put the hole in place first, wait for it to sync, and only then erase
the labels and free the vdev, as vdev_remove_complete() does for an
indirect vdev.  A crash before the labels are erased leaves labels
older than the hole, which the import already prefers to them.
