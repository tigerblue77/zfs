Account a growing non-allocating vdev only once

vdev_metaslab_init() adds the whole space of a top-level vdev with
allocating=off to spa_nonallocating_dspace.  When such a vdev grows,
through a raidz expansion, "zpool online -e" or autoexpand, it is called
again for the new metaslabs and adds the whole new size, without taking
the old size out, so the vdev is accounted twice.

The pool then reports too little available space.  In a test where the
disk was as large as the rest of the pool before it grew, the usable
space of the pool fell to zero.  When the old size exceeds the rest of
the normal class, debug builds fail ASSERT3U(spa_rdspace, >=,
spa_nonallocating_dspace) in spa_update_dspace() and release builds
underflow spa_rdspace.  Setting allocating=on afterwards leaves the old
size counted until the pool is exported and imported.

Take the old size out before the vdev is accounted again with its new
size.
