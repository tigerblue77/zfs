Reload only when the provided config misses top-level vdevs

spa_ld_trusted_config() reloads the pool with the MOS config when the
config it was given has SPA_SYNC_MIN_VDEVS healthy top-level vdevs
fewer than the MOS config, since the latest uberblock may then be on
vdevs that were not opened.  The difference is computed in uint64_t:
a provided config with more healthy top-level vdevs than the MOS
config, as when rewinding to a checkpoint after vdevs were added,
wraps around and also causes a reload, though no vdev was missed.
