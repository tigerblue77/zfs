Record the former guid of a leaf that takes its parent's guid

When a detach leaves a mirror, replacing or spare vdev with a single
child, vdev_remove_parent() puts that child in its place as top-level
vdev and gives it the guid of the parent.  The txg that does so writes
labels 0 and 2 of the device before its uberblocks, so a crash in
between leaves a device whose newest labels name it by the new guid,
while the uberblocks and the MOS config still describe the parent and
the child under its old guid.

An import that scans the devices builds its config from those newest
labels.  vdev_copy_path_relaxed() then looks the leaves of the MOS
config up in it by guid, finds no device for the old guid, and keeps
the path recorded in the MOS config.  When that path no longer leads to
the device, as when device names change across a reboot, no child of
the top-level vdev opens, since the labels of the detached device were
erased before the txg, and the pool fails to import with ENXIO.  It
imports again once the devices are back at their old paths.

"zpool split" has the same window, and gives the label of such a leaf
its former guid as ZPOOL_CONFIG_ORIG_GUID, which vdev_validate()
accepts in place of the guid.  Do so for every leaf that took the guid
of its parent, and look a leaf of the MOS config up by it when its guid
is not found.  Only labels need the key, so leave it out of the MOS
config, which also keeps it from being read back once the change has
synced.  The pool now imports, degraded by the detached device, and so
does a pool left by a crash in the middle of a split, whose labels
already carry the key.  In place, it imports healthy where
vdev_validate() rejected the remaining device for the guid in its
newest label.  Older code already accepts the key in vdev_validate()
and ignores it elsewhere.

Erasing the labels of the detached device only once the detach has
synced would not help on its own: the scan keeps the newest label of
each top-level vdev, which leaves the detached device out of the config
even when its labels are intact.
