## Background

XPOOL: on the owner's host, pools of slow disks writing in bulk slow down a fast pool's reads and synchronous writes. The bench (`run.sh qv`) reproduces it in memory: the fast pool `fastq` (a mirror of two 1 GiB null_blk devices, 4K records) next to three slow raidz2 pools `slow1`-`slow3` (six 128 MiB null_blk devices each under dm-delay, 8 ms), each slow pool taking a 1 MiB sequential writer. Run 2 measures the fast pool's rows rr1 and rr4 (4K random reads, 1 and 4 jobs) and sw1 and sw4 (4K random `O_SYNC` writes) for 20 s each while the slow pools write. The prototype's knob sets are module parameters: `up` (all off), `c1` (`zio_taskq_write` = `sync scale scale null`, sync writes in their own write issue taskq).

TASK-1116-X2 on this VM (16/11, 4 passes, "beyond the spread" = the [min, max] ranges over the passes do not overlap; percentages compare means; baseline = m3w + m3w-up2):

- **m3w-pct25w** (the slow pools re-imported with `zio_taskq_batch_pct=25` and `zio_taskq_write_pct=25`; master's workaround): rr1 IOPS +53 %, p99 −68 %; rr4 IOPS +36 %, p99 −58 %; sw4 IOPS +17 %; no p99 or p99.9 rise on any row; slow-pool MiB/s +7 %.
- **m3w-c1-pct25w** (the same plus c1): rr1 +66 %, rr4 +31 %, sw1 +113 % IOPS, no tail rise. **m3w-c1**: sw1 IOPS +116 %, nothing on rr1 or rr4.
- Calibration m3w against m3w-up2: no cell beyond the spread. Summary for the owner: "c1 et pct25w sans coût".

pct25w changes two things in each slow pool at once: the write issue taskq `s_wr_iss` goes from 6 threads to 2, and the write interrupt taskqs `s_wr_int_0/_1` (2 × 3 threads) become one `s_wr_int` of 2 (the read and free taskqs shrink too). Nobody has measured the issue cut alone. pct25 (batch pct 25, write pct 80) keeps `s_wr_iss` at 6 and cuts the rest; X2 left it out for time.

**The write issue budget** (`zio_taskq_write_budget`, the code under test, read at every dispatch): **0** (default) off, the dispatch path of the prototype without it; **1**: a pool counts as *issuing* from its first gated dispatch to the end of the write passes of the `spa_sync()` that made it; while N pools issue, each runs at most ceil(T / N) gated tasks at once in each of its write issue taskqs of T threads (6 here): 3 slow pools issuing, 2 each (the issue side of pct25w); the fast pool syncing as well (N = 4), still 2 each; a pool issuing alone, all 6. **2**: debugging, not used. Gated: the asynchronous writes of a txg (logical zios dispatched to a write issue taskq, not `ZIO_PRIORITY_SYNC_WRITE`). Never gated: synchronous writes (ZIL, `O_SYNC`), the ZIL's bulk blocks, vdev child I/O. A zio over the limit waits in the gate's list, never a thread. The interrupt taskqs and the thread counts and priorities are unchanged. Its kstat `/proc/spl/kstat/zfs/zio_write_budget` has five counters: `npools` (pools issuing now), and cumulative `gated`, `waited` (gated zios that waited in a list), `npools_sum` (N seen by each gated zio) and `limit_sum` (the limit applied to each). Per row: mean N = d(npools_sum) / d(gated), mean limit = d(limit_sum) / d(gated), waited share = d(waited) / d(gated). With the budget on, `waited` also counts zios a pool alone would have queued in its taskq anyway.

c1 as a default was dropped on 17/11 (TASK-1116-C1B: it cost nothing on z8 and gained only on x1; the ZIL issue track, v5 to v9, was chosen instead). Here c1 stays one of the prototype's knob sets, measured as in X2.

**What changed from TASK-1116-X3 (16/11, never run):** the code is no longer a base64 patch applied to X2's tree: the prototype (`perf-xpool-cause` `6549ab63bd8`) and the budget (`gate.patch`, sha256 `be1b2db1…`, now a commit) were rebased on upstream master `1b1aa0e91b6`, with no conflict outside the workflow files the prototype deletes; the bench as X3 would have run it (run.sh sha256 `b0f33c2d…`), the CPU probe and a driver and report written for this task come from a parentless kit commit.
