## Why

On Linux 5.10 and older, `zvol_open()` reads the `zvol_state_t` from `disk->private_data` and then takes its `zv_state_lock` with nothing holding the zvol in between, and `zvol_os_remove_minor()` relies on `del_gendisk()` to wait for such an opener. That holds on 5.14 and newer (`fops->open()` runs under `disk->open_mutex`, which `del_gendisk()` takes) but not on 5.10 and older, where it runs under the whole disk's `bd_mutex`, which `del_gendisk()` never takes. A fork CI run on AlmaLinux 8 (4.18) panicked this way in `zvol_misc_volmode_race`: `list_del corruption` in `__mutex_remove_waiter()` from `zvol_open()`, in systemd-udevd. The fix `e256741ff14` ("Linux: wait for zvol_open() before freeing a removed zvol") adds a configure check for `bdget_disk()` and `bd_mutex`, and where they exist takes and drops the whole disk's `bd_mutex` right after `del_gendisk()`, before the zvol can be freed. TASK-1120-ZVO checked on 6.12 that the check says `no` there and that the race amplifier is harmless. This task runs the amplifier where the bug lives, on Debian 11's 5.10, with `slub_debug=P`, so that the freed `zvol_state_t` is poisoned (0x6b) and a late `zvol_open()` faults or trips a SLUB or list check instead of quietly using stale memory.

Expected. Master: a finding in dmesg (`general protection fault` at a `0x6b6b...` address, `list_del corruption`, or SLUB `Poison overwritten`), most likely from `zvol_open()` / `__mutex_lock()` in python3 or systemd-udevd. The window is narrow: no finding within the runs is possible, and is reported as "not reproduced" without extra runs. Fix: configure says `yes` on 5.10, so the fix is compiled in, and no finding over runs at least as long as master's whole budget, with the `volmode` churn still going (no deadlock).
