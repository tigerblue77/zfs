## Background

TASK-1120-V9P measured on this VM, against master, "v6" (ZIL block writes issued by the thread that writes them out) and "v9", 6 passes on the disk rows and 12 on the null_blk rows. v6/master: n8 1.153, n8e 1.134, r1 1.169, w4 1.217, w8 1.163, each a gain; x1 1.096, no difference shown; n32 0.946, a loss (p 0.04; V8P 0.972 and V7P 0.966 not significant, V6P 0.944 a loss). On n32, v6 writes `lwbs/op` 0.48 against master's 0.39, `KiB/lwb` 65 against 80, `writer passes/op` 2.19 against 1.97, `fast path %` 23 against 31, in every run since V6P.

Read from the code: while several threads commit to one ZIL, an open lwb waits for more records for `zfs_commit_timeout_pct` (10 %) of a moving average of the lwb latency. Master times that latency from just before the lwb's write enters the zio pipeline to its flush, which includes the wait for a `z_wr_iss` thread; v6 issues inline, so open lwbs wait less. What moved n32 so far: V8P's "v8" (every lwb timed from its close) brought n32 back to `lwbs/op` 0.37 and v8/v6 1.091 (p 0.01) but cost w8 3 % (v8/v6 0.970, p 0.00; its w8 lwbs waited longer too); V9P's "v9" (from the close only when threads waited for `zl_issuer_lock` at the lwb's issue) went halfway on n32 (`lwbs/op` 0.43, v9/v6 1.049, p 0.06) and kept w8; V7P's M1 passes of v6 alone on n32 with `zfs_commit_timeout_pct` at 15 and 20 gave `lwbs/op` 0.41 and 0.35 at 1.06 and 1.13 times master's mean IOPS (single passes). V9P's r1 v9/v6 0.916 (0/6, p 0.03) is not explained by the code: r1 has one writer, so v9's r1 path was v6's plus a `mutex_tryenter`, an atomic load and one `gethrtime` per lwb, and its r1 diagnostics equal v6's (inferred: noise or build layout; it is not repeated in the code below, which has no `tryenter`).

**v10a** and **v10b** are each v6 plus one commit, siblings: neither is built on the other, nor on v8 or v9. Both keep, beside v6's moving average of the latency from the issue, a second one timed from the lwb's close (v8's basis), and an open lwb waits `zfs_commit_timeout_pct` of the one from the close only while a switch says the log is behind its committers, of v6's one otherwise. Nothing else changes; inline issue is as in v6 on every row; no tunable is added.
- **v10a's switch:** at least one commit in 8, over the last window of 1024 commits to that ZIL, found its records already put into an lwb by another thread's writer pass (what `fast path %` counts; V9P's v6: n32 23.3 %, w8 7.0 %, w4 3.7 %, n8 and n8e 0.8 %, r1 and x1 0). - **v10b's switch:** more threads are in `zil_commit()` for that ZIL than there are online CPUs (8 here). n32's 32 jobs exceed it; w8, w4, n8 (2 jobs per zvol), r1 and x1 cannot.

## Written before the run: the rules that decide

- **Verdict per row and pair** (`v10p-report.py raw P`, as V7P to V9P): for a pair a/b, over the passes where both arms ran the row, ratio = a IOPS / b IOPS in the same pass; "gain" if the geometric mean ≥ 1.03 and the exact sign-flip permutation p ≤ 0.05, "loss" if ≤ 0.97 and p ≤ 0.05, otherwise "no difference shown". **"Significantly below"**: geometric mean < 1 and p perm ≤ 0.05, whatever the size of the drop. Pairs: v6/master, v10a/master, v10a/v6, v10b/master, v10b/v6, v10b/v10a.
- **Tail rule** (fsync rows r1, x1, per pair): with t = a fsync p99.9 / b fsync p99.9 per pass, "tail cost" if geo(t) ≥ 1.20 and p perm ≤ 0.05, "tail gain" if geo(t) ≤ 0.83 and p perm ≤ 0.05, otherwise "no tail difference shown".
- **Decision rules, the same for v10a and for v10b** (v below); means are over the passes:
  1. **n32 (fix v6's n32 and lose nothing against master):** v/v6 "gain", and v/master not "significantly below".
  2. **n8, n8e, r1, w4 and w8, each (keep v6's gains):** v/v6 not "significantly below".
  3. **Tails and x1:** on r1 and x1, v/master and v/v6 not "tail cost"; on x1, v/master not "loss".
  4. **Every row:** v's `busy CPU us/op` ≤ 1.03 × max(master's, v6's).

  A variant failing any rule, or with a rule it lacks the data for ("not evaluated"), is reported, not recommended.
- **Choice:** if both meet every rule, v10a (its switch reads the log itself, with no CPU count), unless v10a/v10b is "significantly below" on n32, n8, n8e, r1, w4 or w8, then v10b; if one meets every rule, that one; otherwise none. The report prints each rule's figures and result, a closing line per variant and the choice line; quote them, and recompute none by eye. The **n32 and w8 mechanism lines** under the rules (each variant's `lwbs/op`, `KiB/lwb`, `writer passes/op` and `fast path %`, each against master's and v6's, and which one it is nearer) decide nothing: quote them.
- **Noise:** steal above 1 % on a row is listed with its pass; load1 at the end of a row counts the row's own jobs (n32 runs 32), so it is listed per pass, not judged.
